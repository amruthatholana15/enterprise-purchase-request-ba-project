# Business Requirements

## Objective

The following business requirements were defined based on stakeholder findings, data analysis, root cause analysis, gap analysis, and the proposed TO-BE process.

| ID | Business Requirement |
|---|---|
| BR-01 | The process shall provide requesters with clear purchase requirements based on the selected purchase type. |
| BR-02 | The process shall ensure all applicable mandatory information and supporting documents are provided before a purchase request can be submitted. |
| BR-03 | Every submitted purchase request shall require Manager approval before proceeding to Finance. |
| BR-04 | Finance shall be able to return a purchase request for correction with a documented return reason. |
| BR-05 | The process shall automatically route purchase requests to additional approval stages based on defined business rules. |
| BR-06 | Procurement shall be able to return a purchase request with a clear reason and required corrective action. |
| BR-07 | Requesters shall be able to view the current status and approval stage of their purchase request. |
| BR-08 | Approvers shall receive automated reminders for purchase requests pending beyond defined timelines. |
| BR-09 | Returned purchase requests shall retain previously entered information and clearly identify the information requiring correction. |
| BR-10 | The process shall maintain an audit history of approvals, rejections, returns, corrections and resubmissions. |

## Requirement Prioritization

### High Priority

- BR-01 – Clear purchase requirements
- BR-02 – Pre-submission validation
- BR-03 – Manager approval
- BR-04 – Finance return capability
- BR-06 – Procurement return capability
- BR-07 – Approval status visibility
- BR-09 – Improved return/correction workflow
- BR-10 – Audit history

### Dependent on Business Rules / Configuration

- BR-05 – Conditional approval routing
- BR-08 – Automated reminders and escalation timelines

Exact routing thresholds and reminder timelines would require confirmation from relevant business stakeholders before implementation.
