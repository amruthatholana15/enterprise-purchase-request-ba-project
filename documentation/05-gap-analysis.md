# Gap Analysis

## Objective

The purpose of the Gap Analysis was to compare the current AS-IS purchase request process with the desired TO-BE state and identify improvements required to reduce rework and improve process visibility.

| Area | AS-IS | Identified Gap | TO-BE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Submission Requirements | Requirements are fragmented across Finance, Procurement and general guidance | Requesters do not have one clear view of applicable requirements | Display requirements based on selected purchase type during submission |
| System Validation | ProcureFlow does not consistently enforce all required information and supporting documents | Incomplete requests can enter the approval workflow | Validate mandatory fields and required documents before submission |
| Returns & Rework | Finance and Procurement identify missing or incorrect information after submission | Issues are detected late, creating correction and resubmission cycles | Identify missing information before submission to reduce downstream returns |
| Approval Visibility | Requesters have limited visibility into where requests are pending | Requesters may rely on managers or manual follow-up for updates | Provide current status and approval-stage visibility |
| Notifications | Aging requests may require manual follow-up | Limited automated reminders can allow requests to remain pending | Trigger automated reminders and escalation based on defined timelines |
| Return Handling | Returned requests require correction and resubmission | Requesters need clear guidance on what must be corrected | Display return reason and required corrective action while retaining existing information |
| Approval Routing | Additional approvals depend on business conditions | Routing requirements may vary by amount, purchase type, vendor or compliance conditions | Automatically route requests according to defined business rules |
| Auditability | Workflow actions are recorded across the process | End-to-end history should remain clearly traceable | Maintain approval, return, correction and resubmission history |

## Gap Analysis Conclusion

The primary gap is that submission requirements are not sufficiently standardized and validated at the beginning of the process.

As a result, incomplete or incorrect requests can progress into the workflow and later be returned by Finance or Procurement.

The proposed TO-BE process therefore focuses on improving **upfront validation, requirement clarity, workflow visibility, automated routing, reminders and traceability**.
