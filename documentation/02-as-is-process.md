# AS-IS Purchase Request Process

## Current Process

The existing purchase request process follows the general workflow below:

1. Requester identifies a purchasing need.
2. Requester creates a purchase request in ProcureFlow.
3. Requester enters purchase information and attaches available supporting documents.
4. Request is submitted to the Department Manager.
5. Manager reviews the business need.
6. If approved, the request proceeds to Finance.
7. Finance validates financial information including budget, cost center and purchase amount.
8. If information is incomplete or incorrect, Finance returns the request to the requester for correction.
9. If Finance approves the request, conditional approvals may be triggered based on applicable business rules.
10. The request proceeds to Procurement.
11. Procurement validates purchasing requirements, vendor information, category, quotation and supporting documentation.
12. If information is incomplete or incorrect, Procurement returns the request to the requester.
13. The requester corrects the information and resubmits the request.
14. Applicable approval stages may be repeated depending on the changes made.
15. Once all required approvals are completed, the request reaches final approval.

### Rework Loop

Finance / Procurement  
↓  
Request Returned  
↓  
Requester Corrects Information  
↓  
Resubmission  
↓  
Applicable Approval Stage(s) Repeated

## Current Process Challenges

- Required information and supporting-document requirements are not consistently clear during initial submission.
- Incomplete requests can enter the approval workflow.
- Finance and Procurement identify missing or incorrect information downstream.
- Returned requests create correction and resubmission cycles.
- Requesters have limited visibility into the current approval stage.
- Managers may manually follow up with approval teams.
- Approval reminders and escalations are limited.
- Guidance is fragmented across general, Finance and Procurement documentation.
